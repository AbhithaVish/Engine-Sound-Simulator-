Mounting & testing

  • Secure the phone firmly to the chassis; loose mounting creates false movement readings.
  • Start the engine with the button, then move the RC slowly to test response.
  • Keep the phone speaker openings clear. Try airplane mode to avoid interruptions.
  • This prototype estimates virtual RPM from motion changes. Phone sensors cannot measure true wheel speed, and a steady constant speed may not produce acceleration readings.
